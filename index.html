Our 5 Core AI Automation Services

1. Chatbots

We design and deploy 24/7 intelligent conversational agents that provide immediate, context-aware answers to user inquiries across web and mobile platforms.

Real-World Use Case

An e-commerce store integrates our AI chatbot to resolve 75% of shipping status and refund queries automatically, cutting customer support wait times to instant resolution.

2. Workflow Automation

We link fragmented SaaS software and internal databases into interconnected pipelines that eliminate repetitive copy-pasting and manual data transfers.

Real-World Use Case

A real estate agency automatically routes new web inquiries into CRM software, generates customized property brochures, and alerts the nearest sales broker within 60 seconds.

3. Lead/Email Automation

We implement intelligent email engines that filter inbound leads, evaluate prospect buying signals, and dispatch tailored multi-step follow-up sequences.

Real-World Use Case

A B2B software startup utilizes our email pipeline to personalize cold outreach based on LinkedIn profile data, achieving a 34% reply rate and booking 25 weekly demos.

4. Document Processing

We utilize optical character recognition and large language models to extract key metadata, summarize long records, and classify incoming files automatically.

Real-World Use Case

An accounting firm processes hundreds of monthly vendor invoices by automatically extracting line items, dates, and tax totals directly into accounting software.

5. AI Voice Agents

We develop human-like conversational voice bots capable of conducting inbound and outbound telephone calls with natural cadence and tone variation.

Real-World Use Case

A regional healthcare clinic deploys our voice assistant to manage incoming patient appointment bookings, reducing no-show rates by 42%.

RULES, REGULATIONS & CONFLICT RESOLUTION

Code of professional conduct, conflict mediation protocol, and escalation governance for [visionaries].

Rule 01

Work Hours

Every member must commit 15 hours weekly to agency operations, maintaining core team availability between 2:00 PM and 5:00 PM Monday through Friday.

Rule 02

Communication

Official communication takes place exclusively on Slack and agency email, requiring team members to acknowledge client and peer inquiries within 4 business hours.

Rule 03

Confidentiality

All internal agency designs, customer intellectual property, and proprietary codebase repositories must remain strictly confidential and protected from unauthorized sharing.

Rule 04

Client Data Protection

Client business records must be encrypted in transit and at rest, handled with minimal privilege access, and permanently expunged upon project closure.

Rule 05

Deadlines

Assigned deliverables must be submitted by 11:59 PM on the agreed deadline; any unforeseen blocker must be reported to the Team Lead 24 hours prior.

Rule 06

Ethical AI Use

All AI models must be vetted for bias, respect privacy regulations, and require human-in-the-loop review to guarantee safe and truthful outputs.

Rule 07

Attendance

Attendance is mandatory at all weekly sprint reviews; absences due to academic or personal emergencies require notice to the Team Lead 12 hours in advance.

Rule 08

Quality Checks

No automation system or AI script can be deployed to a client environment without successfully passing peer code review and standardized QA validation.

4-Level Conflict Resolution Process

Structured, progressive resolution framework designed to resolve friction impartially, quickly, and professionally.

1

Level 1: Direct Discussion

Disputing members must hold a private, professional discussion directly within 48 hours to air concerns and reach mutual agreement.

2

Level 2: Team Lead Mediation

If direct talks fail within 48 hours, Team Lead Tanmay Mohod convenes a formal mediation session and records the action plan.

3

Level 3: All 5 Members Vote

If informal mediation cannot achieve compromise, all 5 members cast an equal vote, and the majority decision becomes binding.

4

Level 4: Dean Escalation

As a last resort, serious disputes are formally referred to the Dean of the Branch, whose determination is authoritative and final.
