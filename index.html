Our 5 Core AI Automation Services

1. Chatbots

We design and deploy 24/7 intelligent conversational agents that provide immediate, context-aware answers to user inquiries across web and mobile platforms.
These bots train on company documentation to resolve customer issues accurately and route high-priority tickets to human staff.

Real-World Use Case

An e-commerce store integrates our AI chatbot to resolve 75% of shipping status and refund queries automatically, cutting customer support wait times from 3 hours to instant resolution.

2. Workflow Automation

We link fragmented SaaS software and internal databases into interconnected pipelines that eliminate repetitive copy-pasting and manual data transfers.
Every stage of your business operations triggers the next automatically, ensuring error-free handoffs and continuous team alignment.

Real-World Use Case

A real estate agency automatically routes new web inquiries into CRM software, generates customized property brochures, and alerts the nearest sales broker within 60 seconds.

3. Lead/Email Automation

We implement intelligent email engines that filter inbound leads, evaluate prospect buying signals, and dispatch tailored multi-step follow-up sequences.
The system nurtures prospects on autopilot and directly schedules qualified discovery calls into sales representatives' calendars.

Real-World Use Case

A B2B software startup utilizes our email pipeline to personalize cold outreach based on LinkedIn profile data, achieving a 34% reply rate and booking 25 weekly demos.

4. Document Processing

We utilize optical character recognition and large language models to extract key metadata, summarize long records, and classify incoming files automatically.
This technology converts piles of unstructured PDF forms, receipts, and legal agreements into structured, queryable business databases.

Real-World Use Case

An accounting firm processes hundreds of monthly vendor invoices by automatically extracting line items, dates, and tax totals directly into accounting software without human data entry.

5. AI Voice Agents

We develop human-like conversational voice bots capable of conducting inbound and outbound telephone calls with natural cadence and tone variation.
These agents clarify patient or customer intents, execute appointment bookings, and transcribe conversation highlights into internal management software.

Real-World Use Case

A regional healthcare clinic deploys our voice assistant to manage incoming patient appointment bookings and send telephone reminders, reducing no-show rates by 42%.

I

RULES, REGULATIONS & CONFLICT RESOLUTION

Code of professional conduct, conflict mediation protocol, and escalation governance for [visionaries].

Rule 01

Work Hours

Every member must commit 15 hours weekly to agency operations, maintaining core team availability between 2:00 PM and 5:00 PM Monday through Friday.

Rule 02

Communication

Official communication takes place exclusively on Slack and agency email, requiring team members to acknowledge client and peer inquiries within 4 business hours.

Rule 03

Confidentiality

All internal agency designs, customer intellectual property, and proprietary codebase repositories must remain strictly confidential and protected from unauthorized sharing.

Rule 04

Client Data Protection

Client business records must be encrypted in transit and at rest, handled with minimal privilege access, and permanently expunged upon project closure.

Rule 05

Deadlines

Assigned deliverables must be submitted by 11:59 PM on the agreed deadline; any unforeseen blocker must be reported to the Team Lead 24 hours prior.

Rule 06

Ethical AI Use

All AI models must be vetted for bias, respect privacy regulations, and require human-in-the-loop review to guarantee safe and truthful outputs.

Rule 07

Attendance

Attendance is mandatory at all weekly sprint reviews; absences due to academic or personal emergencies require notice to the Team Lead 12 hours in advance.

Rule 08

Quality Checks

No automation system or AI script can be deployed to a client environment without successfully passing peer code review and standardized QA validation.

4-Level Conflict Resolution Process

Structured, progressive resolution framework designed to resolve friction impartially, quickly, and professionally.

1

Level 1: Direct Discussion

Disputing members must hold a private, professional discussion directly within 48 hours to air concerns, clarify misunderstandings, and reach mutual agreement.

2

Level 2: Team Lead Mediation

If direct talks fail within 48 hours, Team Lead Tanmay Mohod convenes a formal mediation session, assesses both sides, and officially records the meeting notes and action plan.

3

Level 3: All 5 Members Vote

If informal mediation cannot achieve compromise, the issue is brought before the full team. All 5 members cast an equal vote, and the majority decision becomes binding.

4

Level 4: Dean Escalation

As a last resort, serious disputes or deadlock situations are formally referred to the Dean of the Branch, whose executive determination is authoritative and final.

Warning & Penalty Policy

A three-tier disciplinary framework is enforced to ensure project integrity and accountability across all members:

  • Tier 1
    First Violation: Formal verbal warning issued by Team Lead Tanmay Mohod with documented milestones and a 7-day correction timeline.
  • Tier 2
    Second Violation: Official written reprimand, reassignment of critical tasks, and placement on mandatory performance monitoring.
  • Tier 3
    Third Violation: Formal referral to the Dean of the Branch resulting in academic penalties, grade deduction, or dismissal from the agency.

Mandatory Escalation Documentation

To guarantee complete procedural fairness, auditing compliance, and institutional clarity, the following documentation rule is strictly enforced:

Agency Escalation Mandate:
Every single conflict, disciplinary warning, and procedure escalated beyond Level 1 must be officially logged in the agency master registry. Each entry must state the incident timestamp, all involved participants, summary of arguments, evidence reviewed, mediation notes, and the agreed resolution countersigned by the Team Lead and participating parties.
